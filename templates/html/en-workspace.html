Troubleshooting Guide

                                    
Can't connect or requests keep timing out? Check common issues and solutions

                                

                                ▾
                            
                            

                                

                                    

                                        🔒
                                        

                                            System Firewall Blocks
                                            
Mac: System Settings → Network → Firewall → Options, ensure Python/Mock service allows incoming connections.
Win: Check both public and private network options in the firewall popup.

                                        

                                    

                                    

                                        🚫
                                        

                                            AP Isolation & VPN Conflicts
                                            
Network Isolation: Public Wi-Fi (e.g., cafes) often isolates device communication. Solution: Use phone hotspot for PC!
Proxy Conflicts: Close Clash, Shadowrocket, or other proxy apps on the phone.
